# Microscopes and Stains (Examinus)

Everything here is from the Histologic Techniques slides, pp. 91–128. Page numbers are pages of the combined PDF.

## 1. The three microscopes

| | Light microscope (LM) | Transmission electron microscope (TEM) | Scanning electron microscope (SEM) | Page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What focuses the beam | Glass lenses focus light | Magnets focus a beam of electrons | Lenses with an electric coil | 91–93 |
| Top magnification | 1,500 times | 500,000 times | 100,000 times | 91–93 |
| What the picture looks like | Colour section | Grey thin slice, detail inside cells | Grey 3-D surface | 94–96 |

## 2. Preparing the specimen

| Step | LM | TEM | SEM | Page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fixation | Formalin | Formalin, glutaraldehyde, osmium tetroxide | Formalin | 97, 109, 124 |
| Dehydration | Ethanol, xylene | Ethanol | Ethanol | 97, 109, 124 |
| Embedding | Paraffin | Epon, then polymerization | None | 98, 110–111 |
| Cutting | Microtome | Diamond knife; ultrathin sections float in a boat of water | None | 99, 114–115 |
| Support | Glass slide | Copper grid, 3 mm | Metal stub | 101, 116 |
| Stain or coat | Mounting and staining | Lead citrate, uranyl acetate | Critical point drying, then gold sputter coating | 100, 117, 125–126 |

## 3. Parts on the drawings

| Microscope | Parts, in the order the beam meets them | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LM | Illuminator, mirror, light filter, condenser, specimen slide on the stage, objective lenses, prism, eyepiece | 91 |
| TEM | Electron gun (cathode, anode), condenser lens with electric coil, specimen holder with copper grid, objective lens, intermediate lens, projector lens, fluorescent screen, photographic film, CCD camera | 92 |
| SEM | Electron gun (cathode, anode), lenses with electric coil, scanner, specimen at the bottom, detector of electrons, signal amplifier, monitor | 93 |

Only the SEM drawing has a scanner, a detector and a monitor. Only the TEM drawing has a fluorescent screen and a copper grid.
