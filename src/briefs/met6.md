## 6. The respiratory chain and the proton gradient (LO 6)

| 3 | Electron transfer and oxidative phosphorylation | NADH and FADH2 (reduced electron carriers) feed the respiratory chain, O2 becomes H2O, ADP plus Pi becomes ATP |
