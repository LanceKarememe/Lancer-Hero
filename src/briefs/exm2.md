## 3. Collagen synthesis in order

| Step | Where | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alpha chains made with registration peptides; signal peptide clipped | Rough endoplasmic reticulum | 954 |
| Hydroxylation of proline and lysine; vitamin C dependent | Endoplasmic reticulum | 954 |
| Sugars added to hydroxylysine | Endoplasmic reticulum | 954 |
| Triple helix forms: procollagen | Endoplasmic reticulum | 954 |
| Packaging and secretion | Golgi, secretory vesicles | 954 |
| Procollagen peptidases clip the registration peptides: tropocollagen | Outside the cell | 954 |
| Tropocollagen aggregates into fibrils; lysyl oxidase cross-links them | Outside the cell | 954 |

Size order: tropocollagen, fibril, fiber, fiber bundle (p. 954).

## 5. Telling the granulocytes apart

| Cell | On a smear | Key fact | Page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neutrophil | Multilobed nucleus, faint granules; drumstick is the inactive X chromosome | Azurophilic granules: myeloperoxidase, acid hydrolases. Specific granules: proteases, lysozyme | 386–387 |
| Eosinophil | Bilobed nucleus, coarse red-orange granules | Major basic protein in the crystalline internum; attacks parasites | 394–395 |
