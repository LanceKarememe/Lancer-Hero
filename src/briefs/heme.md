## 5. The steps of hemostasis in order (LO 4)

Hemostasis keeps blood fluid yet confined within the vessels, and after injury it runs as six steps (handout, p. 445).

| Step | Name | What happens | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Reflex vasoconstriction | The injured vessel narrows | Reduced blood loss |
| 2 | Platelet adhesion | Within seconds platelets stick to subendothelial collagen, through vWF |  |
| 3 | Contact activation and release | Adherent platelets release mediators that enhance hemostasis (handout) | Hemostasis enhanced |
| 4 | Platelet aggregation | Platelets stick to each other through fibrinogen (handout) | Primary platelet plug: primary hemostasis |
| 5 | Fibrin formation | The coagulation cascade makes thrombin, which converts soluble fibrinogen to insoluble fibrin, over hours (handout) | Definitive plug: secondary hemostasis |
| 6 | Clot retraction | The clot condenses, using platelet actin, myosin and ATP (handout) | Condensed clot |
