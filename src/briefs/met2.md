## 2. Brown versus white adipose (LO 2)

White fat stores and releases fuel; brown fat burns its own fat to make heat (p. 621, p. 622).

**Table 2a. Brown versus white adipose tissue (p. 617, p. 621, p. 622)**

| Feature | White adipose | Brown adipose |
|---|---|---|
| Slide title | Energy storage | Thermoregulation |
| Job | Synthesizes, stores, mobilizes triacylglycerols | Thermogenesis (heat) |
| Trigger for fat breakdown | Hormones (hormone-sensitive lipase) | Cold temperature or large food intake |
| Signal | Glucagon and epinephrine mobilize fatty acids (p. 631, p. 636) | Hypothalamus, then sympathetic nervous system (SNS), then norepinephrine (NE) |
| Fate of the freed fatty acids | Leave the cell; carried in blood on albumin | Beta-oxidation in the cell's own mitochondria |
| Key protein | Hormone-sensitive lipase | Uncoupling protein (thermogenin), also written uncoupling protein 1 (UCP1) |
| Output | Glycerol and fatty acids to blood | Heat, carried away by the capillary |
| Mitochondria | Not drawn | Many, labeled |

**Table 2b. White adipose traffic (p. 621)**

| Step | What happens | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Storage form | Triglyceride stored in a droplet | Fat cell |
| Breakdown | Triglyceride cleaved to glycerol and fatty acids by hormone-sensitive lipase | Fat cell |
| Release | Glycerol and fatty acids enter the capillary | Capillary |
| Transport | Fatty acids bind albumin for transport in blood | Capillary |
| Fat delivery | Chylomicrons and VLDL particles are broken down by lipoprotein lipase | Within the capillary |
| Uptake | The free fatty acids enter the fat cell | Fat cell |
| Backbone | Blood glucose becomes glycerol phosphate | Fat cell |
| Re-storage | Free fatty acids plus glycerol phosphate give triglyceride | Fat cell |

**Table 2c. The two lipases of white fat (p. 621, p. 626, p. 631)**

| Enzyme | Where it acts | What it does | Raised by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hormone-sensitive lipase | Inside the fat cell | Cleaves stored triglyceride | Glucagon (p. 631) |
| Lipoprotein lipase | Within the capillary | Frees fatty acids from chylomicrons and VLDL | Insulin (p. 626) |

**Table 2d. Brown fat activation, in order (p. 622)**

| Step | Event |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cold temperature or large food intake reaches the hypothalamus (HT) |
| 2 | SNS nerves run to brown adipose tissue (BAT) |
| 3 | Nerve endings release NE onto the brown adipose cell |
| 4 | NE binds its receptor (R), then Gs, then adenylyl cyclase (AC) |
| 5 | Cyclic adenosine monophosphate (cAMP) rises |
| 6 | Protein kinase A (PKA) activates a lipase |
| 7 | Triglycerides give free fatty acids (FFA) |
| 8 | FFA undergo beta-oxidation in the mitochondrion |
| 9 | Acetyl-CoA enters the citric acid cycle (CAC); "FADH" and NADH feed the electron transport chain (ETC) |
| 10 | ETC pumps H+ |
| 11 | UCP1 lets H+ back in, releasing HEAT |
| Supply | Capillary brings energy substrates and O2 and carries heat away |

**Table 2e. How uncoupling makes heat (p. 622)**

| Route for H+ back into the matrix | Result |
|---|---|
| Through Fo F1 | ADP + Pi gives ATP |
| Through uncoupling protein (thermogenin) | Heat, no ATP |
