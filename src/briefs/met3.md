## 6. Fates of pyruvate (LO 6)

| Fate | Enzyme | Condition | Organisms and tissues | NAD+ and NADH |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Acetyl-coenzyme A (acetyl-CoA) | Pyruvate dehydrogenase complex | Aerobic | Many tissues | Makes NADH |
| Lactate | Lactate dehydrogenase | Anaerobic | RBC and anaerobic muscle | Uses NADH, regenerates NAD+ |
| Ethanol | Pyruvate decarboxylase, then alcohol dehydrogenase | Hypoxic or anaerobic | Bacteria, yeast, some plants, gut microbiome | Uses NADH, regenerates NAD+ |

**Table 6b. Why pyruvate needs a fate, and the aerobic answer (p. 665)**

| Anaerobic route | Fermentation to lactate |
